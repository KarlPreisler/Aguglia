Welcome

Between the village of Testa dell’Acqua and the hilltop town of Palazzolo Acreide.

Aguglia Persa sits at the heart of the Sicilian countryside, in the hills above Noto. We’re well placed to help you relax in nature while staying close to everything you need.

We live here on the farm, with three dogs and three cats who are all very friendly and will certainly come and say hello. There are two guest rooms, and a large, well-stocked kitchen that everyone shares — perfect for shared meals and stories, and where the good evenings tend to happen.

We don’t serve meals, but you’re welcome to cook, to use the barbecue, and in season to help yourselves from the fruit trees. Every October we harvest our own walnuts and sell them locally.

— Birgitta

The kitchen

Shared by everyone staying, along with the laundry and the pantry.

Our kitchen is your kitchen

It’s big, it’s properly stocked, and it’s for everyone. We don’t serve meals here — with a kitchen like this we’ve never really felt the need to.

Pots and pans, oil, salt and pepper, plates, bowls, cutlery and wine glasses, a fridge, an oven and a hob, coffee in the morning. A dining table long enough for everybody. There’s a laundry room with a washing machine and a drying rack, and a pantry to keep your shopping in.

The rooms

Two guest rooms, each with its own bathroom and its own outdoor space.

Two rooms, and a lounge

Both rooms have a king-size bed, air conditioning and heating, and a bathroom of your own with a toilet, shower and bidet. With only two rooms, you’ll be sharing the house with at most one other party.

Where most evenings end up

Under the pergola there’s a long table and lights strung through the canvas. There’s a barbecue for laid-back cookouts, sun loungers, and plenty of space to dine and unwind in the fresh air.

Our animals

Evenings

When the sun goes down

Around here

A car is essential — there’s no bus up here, and that’s part of the charm.

Baroque towns, gorges, and the sea in half an hour

We’re deep in the countryside but not far from anything. One note before you set off: if your car is low, take our entrance track slowly — it’s 150 metres and it’s bumpy.

  • Testa dell’Acqua5 min

    Our nearest village — bar, bakery, the everyday shopping.

  • Palazzolo Acreide10 min

    A UNESCO-listed Baroque hilltop town, with a Greek theatre cut into the rock above it.

  • Noto25 min

    The cathedral and the honey-coloured Baroque streets. Noto Antica, the abandoned old town, is closer still.

  • Siracusa & Ortigia30 min

    Greek Sicily, and dinner on an island.

  • The coast30–40 min

    Beaches, the Vendicari nature reserve, and Marzamemi’s old tuna-fishing harbour.

  • Cava Grande & PantalicaNearby

    Two great limestone gorges — waterfalls, natural swimming pools and rock-cut tombs. The Bauli caves and the walking paths start almost at our gate.

A few things, for everyone’s wellbeing

  1. We don’t serve meals, but the kitchen is huge and fully equipped and for everyone to use — as is the barbecue area.
  2. Please don’t flush paper or anything else down the toilet. Use the bins provided — it protects our old plumbing and the land around us.
  3. Please don’t leave food unattended, because of the lovely dogs and cats who live here.
  4. No smoking indoors.
  5. Take care of your key — lost keys mean a replacement fee.
  6. We all clean up after ourselves in the shared areas.
  7. Quiet from 11pm.
  8. Please lock the doors when you leave the house.
  9. Most important of all: enjoy yourselves, and feel the peace and harmony here at Aguglia Persa.
