The whole house

4 + 2 guests · both rooms and the lounge · nothing shared with strangers

Take Room 1, the lounge and Room 2 together and the guest side of the farmhouse is yours. The big kitchen, the courtyards, the pergola, the barbecue, the yoga platform and the grounds — with nobody to share them with but us and the animals.

It works best for two families travelling together, or one large one: four in Room 1 and the lounge, two in Room 2. If you want the run of the place for a week in the middle of nowhere, this is the one to ask about.

What you get

  • Both bedrooms, both private bathrooms, and the lounge
  • Sleeps 4 + 2
  • The whole shared kitchen, laundry and pantry to yourselves
  • Two outdoor dining spaces and two outdoor showers
  • Barbecue, pergola, sun loungers and the yoga platform
  • Fireplace in the lounge, lit from October
  • Free parking on the property
  • Cot, highchair, and children’s books and toys if you need them
