Room 2

2 guests · king bed · private bathroom

The same comfy king-size bed, air conditioning and heating, and your own bathroom with a toilet, shower and bidet. Windows on two sides open straight onto the trees, and there is a leather sofa at the foot of the bed for the hot part of the afternoon.

Just outside is your own dining space under the pergola, and your own outdoor shower in the garden. The kitchen, the laundry, the pantry, the barbecue and the grounds are shared with whoever is in Room 1 — and with us, and the dogs and cats.

  • Lock on the door and a private entrance
  • Your own outdoor dining space under the pergola
  • Your own outdoor shower
  • Blackout curtains, hangers, extra pillows and blankets
  • Views over the garden and the hills
